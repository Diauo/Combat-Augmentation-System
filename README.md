# Tushonka-800: Combat Augmentation System

**The T stands for Tushonka. Obviously.**

*Powered by food. Cooled by water. Questionably human.*

Ever wondered what happens when a PMC gets experimental combat implants, questionable R&D funding, and an irresponsible amount of canned meat?

Meet the T-800.

T-800 adds a tactical augmentation system to SPT, giving you access to enemy scanning, directional force shields, and an emergency combat enhancement package.

Because sometimes, surviving Tarkov requires more than good aim, decent armor, and a suspicious amount of painkillers.

Sometimes, you need to become the problem.

## Combat Augmentations

### Target Scanner

**Know your enemy. Preferably before they know you exist.**

Scan hostiles inside a central 16:9 scanning frame. Targets are analyzed individually, with more distant targets taking longer to identify.

- **Clear line of sight:** Scan targets at distances of up to 600 meters.
- **Obstructed targets:** Walls and terrain limit detection to within 50 meters, with significantly slower scanning.
- **Target identification:** Successfully scanned enemies appear as through-wall silhouettes with distance indicators.
- **Continuous tracking:** Targets inside the scanning frame remain tracked, while identification marks last approximately 15 seconds.

No more wondering whether that silhouette is a PMC, a Scav, or your imagination.

Well, mostly.

### Directional Force Shield

**The best firefight is the one where their bullets don't reach you.**

Deploy a three-panel, hexagonal honeycomb shield in front of your character. The shield consists of a central frontal panel and two side wings angled at 45 degrees.

- Physically intercepts incoming enemy projectiles within its protected area.
- Has limited durability and enters cooldown when the shield breaks.
- Your own bullets pass through the shield, allowing you to return fire while protected.

It's not invincibility. It's a highly sophisticated way of making someone else's ammunition your engineering department's problem.

*Note: The shield only protects the directions and areas it actually covers. Getting shot from somewhere else is still very much a possibility.*

### Combat Overdrive

**When the situation is bad, make your body someone else's problem.**

Temporarily pushes the T-800's combat augmentation systems into high-performance operation.

The package provides:

- Health regeneration — unfortunately, blacked-out limbs remain blacked out.
- Pain suppression.
- Increased stamina capabilities.
- Damage reduction.
- Increased carrying capacity.
- Temporary increases to skill levels.

*Side effects may include excessive confidence.*

The enhancement remains active only while sufficient energy is available. Once energy runs out, the system shuts down automatically.

No magic healing. No resurrection. Just a heavily modified human refusing to acknowledge that the fight should probably be over.

## Resource Management

Your augmentations are powerful. Keeping them running is the expensive part.

### Energy — Your Power Supply

Energy is consumed when activating and maintaining skills. Eating replenishes your energy reserves.

If energy falls too low, all active skills shut down and the system disables further skill use until sufficient energy is restored.

Turns out, food is still important even when most of your body has been replaced with questionable military technology.

### Hydration — Your Cooling System

Skills consume hydration while their cooldowns are running. Every skill on cooldown consumes water independently, so running multiple cooldowns simultaneously increases total hydration consumption.

Need your systems back online faster?

Tap **V** while a skill is cooling down to activate **3× cooldown acceleration**. This significantly increases hydration consumption.

If hydration is depleted, accelerated cooldown recovery becomes unavailable, and cooldown duration doubles.

That's right. Your cutting-edge combat implants are water-cooled.

**Stay hydrated. Stay lethal.**

## Controls & Interface

### Controls

- **Hold V:** Open the skill wheel and select an augmentation.
- **Tap V:** Activate the selected skill.
- **Tap V while a skill is cooling down:** Toggle accelerated cooldown recovery.

### Minimalist Holographic HUD

A clean, unobtrusive interface designed to keep your attention on the fight.

- Radial skill-selection wheel.
- Bottom-of-screen skill bar.
- Enemy silhouettes, target markers, and distance indicators.
- Interface language follows the game's language, with Chinese and English support.

No unnecessary tactical furniture. Your screen has suffered enough.

## Configuration

Open the **F12 configuration menu** to customize the system to your liking.

Configurable parameters include:

- Scanner range, scanning time, and related detection settings.
- Shield durability, dimensions, and protection settings.
- Combat enhancement bonuses.
- Hydration thresholds and consumption rates.
- Skill key bindings and other gameplay values.

Tune the system to fit your preferred level of augmentation, from mildly suspicious PMC to walking military procurement violation.

## Technical Information

- **Target:** SPT 4.1.6
- **Framework:** BepInEx client-side plugin
- **Fika:** Compatible
- **Server modifications:** None
- **Profile/save modifications:** None

The entire skill system runs on the client. No server-side installation or save-file changes are required.
